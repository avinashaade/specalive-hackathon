SPECALIVE L1 – TWO-TANK SEQUENCE CONTROLLER

1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW

The system is a demonstration liquid-transfer process consisting of:

- Tank 1: TK-101
- Tank 2: TK-102
- Inlet valve: XV-101 / V1
- Transfer valve: XV-102 / V2
- Drain valve: XV-103 / V3
- Level transmitter for Tank 1: LT-101
- Level transmitter for Tank 2: LT-102
- Sequence controller: PLC-101
- Operator commands:
  - PB-START
  - PB-STOP
  - PB-SHUT

The controller shall automatically execute a fill-transfer-drain sequence.
The operator shall be able to pause/resume the sequence and perform a controlled shutdown.

- TK-101 outlet -> XV-102 inlet
- XV-102 outlet -> TK-102 inlet

- PLC-101 -> XV-102 open command
- PLC-101 -> XV-103 open command


4. NORMAL AUTOMATIC SEQUENCE

When the system is in IDLE and receives START:

1. Enter FILL_T1.
2. Open V1.
3. Fill Tank 1.
4. When Tank 1 level reaches the high-level limit, close V1.
5. Wait for the configured post-fill delay.
6. Enter TRANSFER_T1_T2.
7. Open V2.
8. Transfer liquid from Tank 1 to Tank 2.
9. When Tank 1 reaches its low-level limit, close V2.
10. Wait for the configured post-transfer delay.
11. Enter DRAIN_T2.
12. Open V3.
13. Drain Tank 2.
14. When Tank 2 reaches its low-level limit, close V3.
15. Wait for the configured inter-cycle delay.
16. Start the next automatic fill cycle.


5. CONTROLLER STATES

The intended logical states are:

- IDLE
- FILL_T1
- WAIT_AFTER_FILL
- TRANSFER_T1_T2
- WAIT_AFTER_TRANSFER
- DRAIN_T2
- WAIT_AFTER_DRAIN
- PAUSED
- SHUTDOWN

Legacy simulation names map as follows:

- FILL -> FILL_T1
- HOLD1 -> WAIT_AFTER_FILL
- TRANSFER -> TRANSFER_T1_T2
- HOLD2 -> WAIT_AFTER_TRANSFER
- DRAIN -> DRAIN_T2
- HOLD3 -> WAIT_AFTER_DRAIN

The legacy model does not have an explicit PAUSED state object.


6. START / STOP / SHUT COMMAND BEHAVIOR

Command priority:

SHUT > STOP > START

SHUT:
- Has highest priority.
- A valid SHUT edge immediately abandons stored resume context.
- Enter SHUTDOWN.
- STOP and START do not interrupt an active shutdown sequence.

STOP:
- Has second-highest priority.
- Accepted from normal running and wait states.
- Closes all three valves.
- Enters PAUSED.
- Preserves the interrupted process state.
- If STOP occurs during a wait state, the remaining timer value shall be retained.

START:
- Has lowest priority.
- START from IDLE begins FILL_T1.
- START from PAUSED resumes the interrupted operation.
- START while already running is ignored.
- START during SHUTDOWN is ignored.
- After shutdown completes and the controller returns to IDLE, a new START is required.


7. SHUTDOWN BEHAVIOR

When SHUT is received:

- Enter SHUTDOWN.
- Inhibit V1.
- Open V2.
- Open V3.
- Drain both tanks.
- V2 and V3 are allowed to be open simultaneously during SHUTDOWN.
- Shutdown is complete only when both Tank 1 and Tank 2 are at or below their low-level setpoints.
- Then close V2 and V3.
- Return to IDLE / initial configuration.


8. INTERLOCKS AND SAFETY RULES

- V1 and V2 shall never be open simultaneously.
- During normal automatic operation, V2 and V3 shall not be open simultaneously.
- The V2/V3 simultaneous-open restriction is explicitly waived during SHUTDOWN.
- All valves are de-energize-to-close.
- On controller power loss, valve output commands shall be false.
- Level comparisons include equality:
  - High condition: level >= high limit
  - Low condition: level <= low limit


9. CURRENT EFFECTIVE PARAMETERS

Initial levels:

- Tank 1 initial level = 0.05 m
- Tank 2 initial level = 0.05 m

Current Tank 1 limits:

- Tank 1 high-level limit = 0.80 m
- Tank 1 low-level limit = 0.05 m

Tank 2:

- Tank 2 low-level limit = 0.05 m

Current timing:

- Wait after Tank 1 reaches high level = 10 s
- Wait after Tank 1 reaches low level before opening V3 = 12 s
- Wait after Tank 2 reaches low level before next cycle = 8 s

Nominal flows:

- V1 = 0.006 m^3/s
- V2 = 0.0045 m^3/s
- V3 = 0.005 m^3/s

Controller scan time:

- 0.1 s

Simulation duration:

- 900 s


10. ENGINEERING CHANGE HISTORY

The original URS Rev A specified:

- Tank 1 high limit = 0.78 m
- Wait after Tank 1 low = 10 s
- Wait after Tank 2 low = 10 s

These values were later superseded by approved change CR-004:

- Tank 1 high limit = 0.80 m
- Wait after Tank 1 low = 12 s
- Wait after Tank 2 low = 8 s

CR-004 is the latest approved change and overrides the older values.

The legacy Modelica model predates CR-004 and may contain stale values.


11. INTERFACE SIGNALS

Outputs from PLC:

- XV-102 open command: Boolean
- XV-103 open command: Boolean

Valve commands:

- false = closed

13. ASSUMPTIONS / TRACEABILITY

Important engineering rules:

- Use the latest approved engineering change when conflicting values exist.
- Do not silently choose an older value when a newer approved value exists.
- The 0.78 m Tank 1 high limit is superseded by 0.80 m.
- The 10 s post-transfer delay is superseded by 12 s.
- The 10 s inter-cycle delay is superseded by 8 s.
- The legacy Modelica model may contain obsolete parameters because it predates CR-004.
- STOP during a wait state freezes the remaining wait time.
- SHUT has higher priority than STOP and START.
- The system should explicitly record assumptions and unresolved information rather than inventing missing engineering details.
